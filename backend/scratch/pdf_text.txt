CUSTOMS BOARDING & CLEARANCE CHECKLIST
Standard Operating Procedure for Vessel Inspection & Entry Inward Facilitation
Vessel Name IMO / Registry No.
Nationality / Flag Voyage Number
Port of Berth / Anchor Last Port of Call
Date & Time of Arrival Boarding Officer Name
PHASE 1: PRE-BOARDING ADMINISTRATION & RISK PROFILING
✔ INSPECTION PARAMETER VERIFICATION & COMPLIANCE NOTES STATUS /
REMARKS
Manifest Verification Verify Sea Arrival Manifest (SAM) or Import
General Manifest (IGM) data filed on the customs
portal.
Vessel Risk Assessment Review previous compliance history, flags, and
blacklists for high-risk origins/routes.
PPE & Safety Readiness Ensure mandatory boarding gear (safety boots,
high-vis jacket, tactical flashlight, radiation pager)
is active.
PHASE 2: PHYSICAL BOARDING & DOCUMENT AUDIT
✔ INSPECTION PARAMETER VERIFICATION & COMPLIANCE NOTES STATUS /
REMARKS
Master Interview & Log ReviewExamine the Deck Logbook and Master’s Personal
Log to cross-verify port arrival timelines and route
track.
Statutory Declarations CollectCollect duplicate signed copies of General Dec,
Crew List, Vessel Store List, and Maritime
Declaration of Health.
Crew Property Declarations Crosscheck individual Crew Private Property
Declarations (cash/currency, personal high-value
electronics).
Official Use Only - Customs & Maritime Operations Page 1 of 3
PHASE 3: BONDED STORE AUDITING & SEALING
✔ INSPECTION PARAMETER VERIFICATION & COMPLIANCE NOTES STATUS /
REMARKS
Bonded Inventory Audit Physically count high-risk excise commodities
(liquor bottles, cigarettes, commercial tobacco
stocks).
Customs Seal Application Affix official Customs physical seals on bonded
lockers. Record the precise seal numbers in the
report.
Controlled Substance Audit Audit the ship’s medical locker for narcotics,
psychotropics, and restricted medical drugs.
Ensure safe lockup.
Arms & Ammunition Check Verify anti-piracy weapons, ammunition rounds,
and regulatory safe-keep holdings against
declarations.
PHASE 4: PHYSICAL VESSEL & TECHNICAL VERIFICATION
✔ INSPECTION PARAMETER VERIFICATION & COMPLIANCE NOTES STATUS /
REMARKS
Load Line / Plimsoll Marks Visually check draft marks and Plimsoll lines to
prevent illicit over-weighting or unauthorized cargo
drops.
Bunker & Fuel Logs Check Oil Record Book (ORB) entries and cross-
verify heavy fuel oil (HFO) / marine gas oil (MGO)
remaining volumes.
Spot Quarter Inspection Execute random physical spot checks (min 5%) of
crew cabins to intercept undeclared contraband or
contraband pools.
PHASE 5: CLEARANCE AUTHORIZATION & SIGN-OFF
✔ INSPECTION PARAMETER VERIFICATION & COMPLIANCE NOTES STATUS /
REMARKS
Discrepancy Evaluation Identify and formalize any shortfalls or undeclared
assets. Issue fine/detention warnings if necessary.
Grant Inward Entry Sign off on the physical boarding document to
officially authorize the start of shore cargo
unloading.
